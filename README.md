# Clnch Web

**Clnch | Your AI Opportunity Assistant**

The marketing website and product concept demo for **Clnch**, an AI opportunity assistant designed to help people move from discovering an opportunity to actually pursuing it.

CLNCH sits between the complexity of the web and the person trying to navigate it: helping them find, understand, prepare for, and complete opportunities such as scholarships, jobs, fellowships, grants, accelerators, internships, and university programs.

**Live:** https://clnchweb.vercel.app

---

## What is Clnch?

Finding an opportunity is only the beginning.

The difficult part often comes afterward:

- Understanding what the opportunity actually requires
- Figuring out whether you're eligible
- Finding the right application page
- Interpreting confusing requirements and terms
- Gathering the information and documents you need
- Keeping track of deadlines
- Answering application questions
- Preparing responses
- Completing repetitive parts of applications
- Knowing what to do next

**Clnch is being built to stay with you through that process.**

The core idea is:

> **OPPORTUNITY → CLNCH → HUMAN**

Instead of simply giving people another list of opportunities, CLNCH acts as an intelligent layer between the opportunity and the person pursuing it.

### The journey

**FIND → UNDERSTAND → PREPARE → COMPLETE**

CLNCH can help investigate an opportunity, explain what matters, assess fit against your profile, track deadlines, answer questions, prepare application materials, and eventually assist with repetitive application tasks.

The goal isn't to promise that you'll get the opportunity.

The goal is to make sure **the process doesn't become the reason you never try.**

> **You can't know if you'll get an opportunity until you take your shot.**

## Product Concept

CLNCH is designed around a simple principle:

> **Don't just find it. Finish it.**

The product is being explored as an AI assistant that can work alongside a user while they navigate opportunities across the web.

### CLNCH Lens

A browser-based assistant that can understand the page you're currently viewing.

For example:

> "What does this requirement mean?"

CLNCH can interpret the relevant information, explain it in simpler language, and help the user understand what to do next.

### Opportunity Intelligence

CLNCH can extract important information from opportunity pages, including:

- Deadline
- Eligibility
- Requirements
- Location
- Funding
- Application links
- Important conditions
- Other relevant details

The goal is to turn messy opportunity pages into information that is easier to act on.

### Personal Fit

CLNCH can use a user's opportunity profile, including: education, experience, skills, interests, goals, location, and career direction — to help answer:

**"Is this actually relevant to me?"**

The emphasis is on transparent reasoning rather than an unexplained AI score.

### Opportunity Watch

Some opportunities close and reopen periodically.

CLNCH can conceptually monitor opportunities a user cares about and notify them when relevant application windows open again.

### Application Assistance

CLNCH is designed to eventually help users:

- Understand application questions
- Retrieve information from their profile
- Prepare answers
- Draft responses in their own voice
- Reuse information they have already provided
- Navigate repetitive application steps
- Review an application before submission

For consequential actions such as submitting an application, **the human remains in control.**

### Page & Terms Intelligence

CLNCH can help users understand long or confusing pages by surfacing what matters — such as requirements, deadlines, obligations, fees, and data-related terms.

CLNCH is intended to make information easier to understand, not replace professional or legal advice.

## Product Philosophy

CLNCH is built around a simple observation:

**The opportunity isn't always the problem. The process is.**

People often encounter an opportunity and then stop because the process feels confusing, time-consuming, intimidating, or difficult to navigate.

CLNCH is designed to reduce that friction.

It should help people move from:

**"I found this."**

to:

**"I understand this."**

to:

**"I'm ready."**

to:

**"I submitted it."**

Clnch doesn't decide whether someone will succeed.

It helps them **take the shot.**
